# Daily Digest -- 2026-06-16

**Focus:** AI-in-Education research scan from arXiv cs.CY, cs.HC, Semantic Scholar, and OpenAlex.

## New Papers Ingested

- **Improving Capstone Team Outcomes through Dynamic Skill Matching and Preference Alignment** -- Pardi, Castro, Pisman, Adhikari & Chandrasekhar -- arXiv:2606.15572 -- LLM-driven team formation for capstone projects outperforms CATME Team-Maker in skill coverage and preference alignment [[dynamic-skill-matching-capstone-teams]]
- **LearnOpt: Recovering the Latent Cognitive Structure of Standardized Examinations via Knowledge Graphs and Constrained Optimization** -- Bose & Thomas -- arXiv:2606.15349 -- Standardized exams have stable latent cognitive structures recoverable via LLM-tagged question analysis and knapsack optimization [[learnopt-exam-cognitive-structure]]
- **"Stuck in a Spiral": Shame and Guilt as Social Regulators of AI Use in Computing Education** -- Hamilton, Hou, Patel, Nnam, Patel & MacNeil -- arXiv:2606.14920 -- Shame and guilt create cycles of hiding and moral tension around AI use in computing education [[shame-guilt-ai-regulation-computing-education]]
- **Gender Differences in AI Literacy Workshop Outcomes and Deepfake Engagement** -- Renzella, Bergh, Banks & Vassar -- arXiv:2606.14718 -- Single-day AI literacy workshops partially narrow gender STEM gaps; males need targeted deepfake safety education [[gender-differences-ai-literacy-deepfake]]
- **AI as a Partner in Learning about, Doing, and Engaging with Science: Vigilance as the Key to Productive Augmentation** -- Kubsch -- arXiv:2606.16822 -- Epistemic vigilance determines whether AI augments or undermines learning; uniform AI integration risks widening achievement gaps [[ai-partner-science-epistemic-vigilance]]
- **Measuring Whether LLM Tutors Teach or Solve: A Diagnostic for Educational Impact** -- Yao, Zheng & Li -- arXiv:2606.16206 -- Benchmarks conflate solving with teaching (r=0.421); pedagogy and solving scores should be reported separately [[measuring-llm-tutors-teach-vs-solve]]
- **Cross-Dataset Bloom Question Classification: Supervised Models and Prompted LLMs** -- Faraji, Molavi, Rasoulkhani, Tavakoli & Kismihok -- arXiv:2606.13684 -- LLMs with tailored prompting generalize better than supervised models for cross-dataset Bloom taxonomy classification [[cross-dataset-bloom-question-classification]]
- **Using AI in engineering education: a balancing act, driven by clear purpose** -- Kudina -- arXiv:2606.16626 -- Students attachment to LLM efficiency reflects 'cruel optimism' - benefits depend on critical thinking skills they are still developing [[ai-engineering-education-balancing-act]]
- **Are LLM-based Chatbots Good Enough to Support Computer Science Students in Multiple-Choice Exercises?** -- Stamatakis, Gavali, Berger, Wartena, Hoppe & Ewerth -- arXiv:2606.15919 -- ChatGPT answers with explanations do not improve student MCQ performance; GPT-4o/5 outperform smaller models [[llm-chatbots-cs-multiple-choice]]
- **The Missing Layer: Why EdTech Needs Design-Time Generative UI, Not Just Runtime Personalization** -- Neshaei, Shibu & Gures -- arXiv:2606.15902 -- Design-time GenUI with card-based semantic units embeds UDL and eliminates per-learner inference costs [[edtech-design-time-generative-ui]]
- **What do you mean by human-AI collaboration: Prerequisite functions and the affordances needed to achieve it** -- Cukurova -- arXiv:2606.15509 -- 5-level taxonomy: most human-AI interaction is consultation/delegation, not true collaboration [[human-ai-collaboration-prerequisite-functions]]
- **Leveraging Physiological Signals to Predict Exam Outcomes with Machine Learning** -- Yamazaki & Rimal -- arXiv:2606.14960 -- Random forest predicts exam outcomes from physiological signals as well as deep learning with better interpretability [[physiological-signals-exam-outcomes-ml]]
