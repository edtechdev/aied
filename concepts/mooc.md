> **Massive Open Online Courses (MOOC)** — large-scale online courses designed for open, unlimited participation, typically delivered through a platform to thousands of learners. MOOCs are a canonical form of [[online-teaching-and-learning|online teaching and learning]], known for reach and scalability but historically limited in adaptivity and personalization.

The MOOC format excels at scale — serving thousands of learners with pre-recorded, structured content — but has struggled to personalize instruction. Generative AI and [[agentic-ai|agentic AI]] systems (see [[mooc-to-maic|MAIC]]) are being explored as a way to combine MOOC scalability with adaptive, personalized support, shifting from "one video for N students" toward "N agents for 1 student." MOOC learners' [[academic-integrity|academic integrity]] and [[cognitive-offloading|cognitive offloading]] risks are also heightened by the self-paced, often unproctored nature of the medium.
