# GenAI as a runaway object in higher education: A socio-cultural view on AI-influenced academic practice in mathematics

**Authors:** Dionysia Bakogianni, Yvonne Liljekvist, Phuong Bui
**Source:** [Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, Vol 11, 100637](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100637) — Open Access (CC BY 4.0)

## Key Findings

Qualitative study of 10 mathematics academics at a Swedish university, using Cultural-Historical Activity Theory (CHAT). Identifies 5 themes of GenAI-driven change: boundary fluidity, shifting objects, epistemic concerns, distributed innovation, and reconfigured roles. Adoption unfolds informally through student use and peer networks rather than institutional policy. GenAI is conceptualized as a 'runaway object' that evolves unpredictably and disrupts established boundaries.
