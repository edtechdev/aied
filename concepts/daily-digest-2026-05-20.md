### [[cognitive-shift-ai-education]] — Evidence of a Cognitive Shift in AI Education: How Students Are Rethinking Human Intelligence?
**Rekik (2026)** — ICLR HCAIR Workshop. 471 students over 6 years show a shift from AI preference to human intelligence preference (65–90% by 2026). Four phases: hype, distrust, trust, dependency. Design-oriented courses accelerate this cognitive shift more than technical courses.

### [[vocabulary-difficulty-prediction]] — What Makes Words Hard? Sakura at BEA 2026 Shared Task on Vocabulary Difficulty Prediction
**Nohejl et al. (2026)** — BEA 2026. LLM fine-tuned with soft-target loss achieves r>0.91 for vocabulary difficulty prediction. Explainable model reveals spelling and test item construction affect measured difficulty beyond word frequency. Open-source code available.

### [[engagement-forecasting-its]] — From Heuristics to Analytics: Forecasting Effort and Progress in Online Learning
**Qiu et al. (2026)** — EDM 2026. 15 models benchmarked on 425 middle-school students. Feature-based forecasting reduces MAE 22–33% vs. heuristics. Effort and progress have distinct predictive signatures. Tutor interviews confirm practical utility for goal-setting.
