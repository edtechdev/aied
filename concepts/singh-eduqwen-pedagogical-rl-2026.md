> **EduQwen: Pedagogical RL** — We present a multi-stage optimization strategy combining reinforcement learning (RL) and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) to enhance pedagogical knowledge of LLMs, producing EduQwen 32B-RL1, EduQwen 32B-SFT, and EduQwen 32B-SFT-RL2. These are application-driven open-source pedagogical LLMs built on a dense Qwen3-32B backbone.
