## Key Themes
1. **Metacognition crisis**: Two papers (Fernandes, Gibson) independently identify metacognitive calibration failures as critical barriers — reasoning traces inflate confidence without improving performance, and metacognitive theory lacks operationalized taxonomies for AI systems.
2. **Governance and moderation**: Gao et al. provide first principled framework for GenAI in assessment, while Cho et al. identify why AI productivity promises often fail — together they suggest that technical capability is insufficient without deliberate human-systems design.
3. **Digital literacy illusion**: Rodriguez-Alvarez et al. empirically validate that the "Digital Native" myth is dangerous — students with high self-efficacy have low actual AI skills, demanding hands-on pedagogical reform.
4. **Uncanny AI**: Yin et al. demonstrate that humanlike AI design in writing tools creates a social uncanny valley — efficiency gains come with surveillance discomfort.
