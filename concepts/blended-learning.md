> **Blended learning** — an instructional model that combines in-person and [[online-teaching-and-learning|online]] components, intentionally integrating digital activities, materials, and interactions with face-to-face teaching. Blended (and hybrid) formats are a core setting for AI in education.

Blended learning asks instructors to decide what is best done synchronously vs. asynchronously and online vs. in person — decisions that [[instructional-design]] principles organize and that AI both supports and complicates. In the blended context, AI tools ([[intelligent-tutoring|tutoring agents]], adaptive platforms, discussion facilitation) offer opportunities for personalization and always-on support, while raising [[academic-integrity|academic integrity]] and [[cognitive-offloading|cognitive offloading]] risks that span both the online and in-person portions.
