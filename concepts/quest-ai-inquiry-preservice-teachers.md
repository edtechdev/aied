# Effects of an AI-supported inquiry model on AI literacy and authentic performance: A quasi-experimental study with preservice teachers

**Authors:** Dongyun Cao, Yuting Yan, Anyuan Xiong, David Wicks
**Source:** [Computers and Education: AI, Vol 11](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100630) — Open Access (CC BY 4.0)

## Key Findings

Quasi-experimental study with 95 preservice teachers over 10 weeks. QUEST+AI model (Question, Understand, Engage, Solve, Teach) integrated with coached GenAI use. Experimental group showed higher overall AI literacy (largest gains in AI application, problem-solving, and emotion regulation). Also earned higher research proposal scores (partial η²=.137). Structured AI coaching can build AI literacy while improving discipline-relevant performance.
