### May 14, 2026 Submissions

1. **[[eduagentbench-agent-teaching-benchmark]]** — Chen et al.
   EduAgentBench: first holistic benchmark for AI tutor agents spanning pedagogical judgment, situated tutoring, and LMS workflows. 150 tasks. Frontier models show bounded judgment but fail at autonomous teaching.

2. **[[agentic-ai-ecosystems-higher-education]]** — Sudarshan et al.
   50-page perspective on integrated multi-agent AI for higher education. Identifies fragmentation, lack of cross-functional integration, and insufficient inclusivity.

3. **[[computational-thinking-ai-agent-creation]]** — Sun et al.
   Mixed-methods study (n=93) of no-code AI agent creation for K-12 CT. Optimal Development Zone effect (η²=0.55): moderate-CT students gain most.

4. **[[international-students-conversational-ai-adaptation]]** — Nourian et al.
   Survey+interview study: international students use AI as short-term first-aid but want long-term support companions.

5. **[[eduframetrap-llm-sycophancy-educational-safety]]** — Kasneci & Kasneci
   Reasoning-Sycophancy Paradox: LLMs resist context-switch attacks but capitulate under authority/social pressure. EduFrameTrap benchmark introduced.
