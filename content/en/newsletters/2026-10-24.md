## Main highlights and questions

> Both of this week's headline results say the same thing: a score is not the outcome. [A three-arm trial](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/bassner-performance-learning-dissociation-ai-programming-2026/) lifted exercise scores from 29.85% with the open web to 71.84% with unrestricted ChatGPT while conceptual learning did not differ at all, so a higher mark under AI access is not evidence that students learned more. [Thirteen detectors](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/park-style-confound-ai-detection-non-native-writing-2026/) flagged human-written manuscripts at rates from 0% to 100% after a single copy-edit, so a detector flag is not evidence of misconduct.

- [Does the tutor that lifts the homework also lift the exam?](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-proof-learning-cognitive-offloading-2026/) A randomized trial put a proof tutor in front of students and then measured an exam taken without it.
- [When a submission looks fine, how much of it is the model's?](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/llm-math-homework-offloading-traces-2026/) Fifty-nine chatlogs, compared against the work the same students handed in.
- [How often does an AI detector flag writing a human actually wrote?](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/park-style-confound-ai-detection-non-native-writing-2026/) Thirteen detectors, 135,389 document pairs where only the language changed.
- [Is AI speaking practice improving speaking, or one measured feature of it?](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/chatbots-oral-proficiency-efl-systematic-review/) Ten studies, six CEFR features, and four studies that reported a single holistic score.
- [Does teaching students to use feedback build their engagement, or the other way round?](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-feedback-literacy-agentic-engagement-panel-2026/) A three-wave panel that cannot order the two.
- [Where do AI tutors still fail when a student refuses, withdraws, or escalates?](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/tutor-case-bench-challenging-behaviors-2026/) 923 dialogues built from case studies of real students, scored against what practitioners documented.
- [Is AI governance in education law, guidance, or aspiration?](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/generative-ai-governance-education-scoping-review-2026/) 160 documents, of which four take binding form.

## For instructors

### [A tutor can lift the homework and leave the exam where it was](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-proof-learning-cognitive-offloading-2026/)

[Chen and colleagues](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-proof-learning-cognitive-offloading-2026/) randomized 153 proof-writing students at Carnegie Mellon to get a proof tutor early or late, then measured an exam taken with no AI in the room. Homework scores rose 2.56 points while the tool was available. Midterm scores did not differ. Once everyone had access and chose their own dose, every 100 additional chatbot messages went with a 3.02-point lower score on the next exam, and students with lower baseline [self-efficacy](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/self-efficacy/) were the ones who used it most.

What that changes for you: a grade earned with the tutor open is not evidence of what a student can do without it, so the unassisted task has to carry that weight. It also means the students most at risk of [leaning on it](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/cognitive-offloading/) are the ones least confident going in, which is visible in usage before it is visible in scores.

### [Unrestricted chat inflates scores more than a well-built tutor does](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/bassner-performance-learning-dissociation-ai-programming-2026/)

In a [three-arm trial](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/bassner-performance-learning-dissociation-ai-programming-2026/) at a Munich CS1 course, 275 students spent 90 minutes on a Java exercise with a hint-giving tutor, with unrestricted ChatGPT, or with the open web. Exercise scores came out at 71.84%, 57.50% and 29.85%. Conceptual knowledge gains were 0.83, 0.71 and 0.85 points, and the difference between conditions was nowhere near significant (p = .773). Both AI conditions reduced frustration; only the tutor raised [motivation](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/motivation/).

The score spread is mostly about the artifact, not the learning. If you grade the exercise, the AI conditions are being graded on a different task.

### [Teacher-built chatbots fail at intent, not facts](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/eduwild-teacher-configured-chatbots-failures-2026/)

[EduWild](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/eduwild-teacher-configured-chatbots-failures-2026/) collected 1,726 conversations from 217 teacher-configured Playlab bots, including 900 from the students they were deployed to. Bots skipped required workflow steps in 53.8% of end-user conversations, never established their role in 33.4%, and overloaded learners with information or questions in 37.1%.

Before you deploy a bot you built or inherited, read its first turn and check that it states what it is and what happens next. The faults documented here are in the instructions, and they are fixable in the [prompt](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/prompt-engineering/) without touching the model.

### [A detector verdict is not evidence](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/park-style-confound-ai-detection-non-native-writing-2026/)

An editing-service dataset gave [Park and colleagues](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/park-style-confound-ai-detection-non-native-writing-2026/) 135,389 document pairs that hold author, topic, and content fixed while only the language changes. Across 13 detectors, false positives on human-written academic text ran from 0% to 100%, and the same edits moved detectors in opposite directions. Separately, a group of sessional markers [lost their detector entirely](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/sessional-academics-contract-cheating-digital-misconduct-2026/) because it flagged grammar checkers, and now read for fabricated references and disconnection from course materials instead.

If you have been putting a detector score in front of a student, especially a student writing in a second language, this is the week to stop [using detectors](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/ai-detection/). The reading-based signals in the second study are the practical alternative.

### [Assessment design is a choice among three constraints, not a search for a principle](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/assessment-feedback-trilemma-model-2026/)

[Pedlow, Fischer, and Williams](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/assessment-feedback-trilemma-model-2026/) argue the problem is not a shortage of design principles but the absence of a way to reason about the ones that pull against each other. Their dual trilemma treats [authenticity](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/authentic-assessment/), observability, and feasibility in assessment, and specificity, timeliness, and workload in feedback, as constraints you trade off deliberately.

Asked to redesign an assessment for AI, that framing turns an open question into a short argument you can record and defend.

### [Speaking gains from chatbots are uneven by feature](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/chatbots-oral-proficiency-efl-systematic-review/)

[Soriano-Gonzalez and Belda-Medina](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/chatbots-oral-proficiency-efl-systematic-review/) screened 306 records down to ten studies and asked which of the six CEFR features of spoken language actually moved. Range and intelligibility improved in four studies each, coherence in one, interaction in almost none. Four of the ten reported a single holistic score, so their gains cannot be traced to a feature at all.

A vendor claim of "improved speaking" is compatible with a change the study never measured. For listening, [Goh and Aryadoust find the evidence much thinner](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-second-language-listening-speaking-assessment-2025/).

### [Feedback literacy and engagement feed each other](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-feedback-literacy-agentic-engagement-panel-2026/)

[A three-wave panel](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-feedback-literacy-agentic-engagement-panel-2026/) of 399 undergraduates found [AI feedback literacy](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/feedback-literacy/) predicting later agentic engagement (β = 0.222) and the reverse path just as strong (β = 0.263), with no statistical basis for ordering the two.

Teaching students to read and use feedback is not a prerequisite you finish before engagement starts. The two move together, so a course that only builds one of them is working against itself.

## For developers

### [Hard cases are where tutors still fail, and strategy retrieval helps mid-tier models](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/tutor-case-bench-challenging-behaviors-2026/)

[TutorCaseBench](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/tutor-case-bench-challenging-behaviors-2026/) turned [qualitative case studies](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/qualitative-research/) into 923 six-turn dialogues across five challenge types and scored 11 models against case-specific rubrics. The best model satisfied a documented intervention in 55.8% of open-ended cases, which is the number to design against. Supplying the case literature's documented strategy lifted 10 of 11 models by an average of 9.9 points, with Llama 4 Maverick moving from 41.0% to 64.8%, while the strongest model stayed flat at 71.3%.

Retrieval of reference strategies is worth building for the models you can afford. It will not rescue a frontier model that already has the pattern.

### [Validate against deployed conversations, not lab prompts](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/eduwild-teacher-configured-chatbots-failures-2026/)

[The EduWild dataset](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/eduwild-teacher-configured-chatbots-failures-2026/) pairs every conversation with the [system prompt](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/prompt-engineering/) and settings behind it, which is what makes the failure analyzable: 53.8% of end-user conversations skipped a required step. Teacher-authored configuration is a distinct failure surface from model capability, and a prompt-level harness recovered intent without removing the teacher's control.

If your product lets teachers write the instructions, your test set has to be their instructions, collected after deployment.

### [The rubric dimension of feedback quality is the one nobody measures](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/evidence-grounded-framework-ai-generated-educational-feedback-2026/)

[Yang examined 68 studies](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/evidence-grounded-framework-ai-generated-educational-feedback-2026/) of AI-generated feedback. Evidence validity appears in 47 of them, pedagogical usefulness in 58, and rubric and task alignment in 19. [Alignment](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/ai-feedback-quality/) is the dimension a product can most directly control, and it is the least studied.

That is an opening: build against the rubric the assignment already uses, and say so, because the published evidence will not tell buyers whether anyone else does.

### [Actionability is a design target, not a nicety](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/voice-based-ai-feedback-interviews-higher-ed-2026/)

[Tamme, Schilling, and Hantel](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/voice-based-ai-feedback-interviews-higher-ed-2026/) propose replacing the end-of-semester written [survey](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/self-report-measures/) with a phone interview run by an AI agent, on the argument that the written instrument fails on response rate, depth, and actionability. It is a proposal rather than a field test. The design claims are specific enough to borrow: ask follow-ups, require a reason, return something the recipient can act on.

## For researchers

### [Self-report and performance often reach different conclusions](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/same-construct-same-conclusion-self-report-vs-performance-2026/)

[Fan and colleagues](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/same-construct-same-conclusion-self-report-vs-performance-2026/) mapped measurement methods across 2,099 records, then examined 51 cases where a [self-report instrument](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/self-report-measures/) and a test, rating, or trace measured the same construct. The answer to whether the two agree is often no. Where they diverge, the construct's [observability](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/assessment-validity/) explains it better than study quality does.

Substituting one for the other is a measurement decision with a direction, not a convenience. Report which one your claim rests on.

### [Artifacts hide offloading, so measure the process](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/llm-math-homework-offloading-traces-2026/)

[Kontorovich and Wallach](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/llm-math-homework-offloading-traces-2026/) coded the 429 prompts in 59 chatlogs as [self-regulation](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/self-regulated-learning/) traces and compared each against the submitted work. Ten of the fifteen students who completed all three tasks changed which role they gave the model as the task changed, yet 53 of 59 submissions reproduced the model's product or revised it only in form.

A submission alone cannot distinguish use from [substitution](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/cognitive-offloading/). Process data can, and the coding scheme here is reusable.

### [A validated scale for reliance in open-ended tasks](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/genai-reliance-scale-problem-solving-2026/)

Most reliance measures infer dependence from whether a student followed correct advice, which needs a checkable answer. [Hou and colleagues](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/genai-reliance-scale-problem-solving-2026/) built and validated a 16-item scale for [problem-based learning](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/problem-based-learning/), where no such answer exists.

### [Detector benchmarks decay, so report the adversary](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ren-test-time-adaptation-ai-text-detection-2026/)

[Ren, Raghavan, and Garg](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ren-test-time-adaptation-ai-text-detection-2026/) separate the [detection question](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/ai-detection/) into separability and motion. Supervised baselines separate human from machine prose almost perfectly in distribution, while the commercial detector Pangram fell to 63.5% balanced accuracy and 27% recall against a prompt written to evade it.

Numbers reported without the evasion attempt describe a world that no longer exists. And the review literature is thin where it matters: of 51 studies of GenAI feedback on second language writing, [5 check whether the feedback is accurate](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/genai-l2-written-feedback-scoping-review-2026/).

## For administrators

### [Most education AI governance is guidance, not law](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/generative-ai-governance-education-scoping-review-2026/)

[Elmahjub and colleagues](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/generative-ai-governance-education-scoping-review-2026/) read 160 English-language [governance](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/governance/) documents closely, from 498 records. Only 4 take formal legal or regulatory form, while 92 are guidance, frameworks, toolkits, or handbooks. Accountability appears in 146 documents but is paired with a named oversight actor in 100.

A policy without a named owner and a mechanism is a statement of intent. The review gives you the fields to audit your own document against.

### [Detector procurement needs a false-positive number](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/park-style-confound-ai-detection-non-native-writing-2026/)

Thirteen detectors, human-written academic text as the control, [false positives from 0% to 100%](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/park-style-confound-ai-detection-non-native-writing-2026/). At least one institution has already retired its detector because it flagged grammar checkers. If a vendor will not give you a false-positive rate on human work in your own discipline, you cannot price the [disciplinary risk](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/academic-integrity/).

### [The documented direction in assessment is process, authenticity, and oral work](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/higher-ed-assessments-genai-systematic-review/)

[Crompton and Burke](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/higher-ed-assessments-genai-systematic-review/) review 72 studies from 24 countries and 20 disciplines and find three design responses recurring: [process-oriented](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/process-oriented-assessment/), [authentic](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/authentic-assessment/), and [oral assessment](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/oral-assessment/). The evidence base is wide and methodologically thin, most studies cover a single course, and none of that changes the direction.

### [Adoption has outrun understanding among teachers](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-misconceptions-indonesian-k12-teachers-2026/)

Among [132 Indonesian K-12 teachers](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/ai-misconceptions-indonesian-k12-teachers-2026/), 96.2% support teacher use of AI and 84.6% support student use, yet 69.2% gave a vague or mistaken account of what a chatbot does with an instruction.

Support in a survey is not capability in a classroom. The [professional development gap](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/teacher-ai-competency/) here shows up before any tool deployment does, and the instrument is short enough to reuse as a needs assessment.

### [Replace the course survey only if the replacement is actionable](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/voice-based-ai-feedback-interviews-higher-ed-2026/)

The [voice interview proposal](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/voice-based-ai-feedback-interviews-higher-ed-2026/) targets the failure modes that make end-of-term [surveys](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/self-report-measures/) useless: response rate, depth, satisficing, and answers nobody can act on. Treat it as a specification for whatever you use instead, not as evidence that the AI version works.

## Concept spotlight

Four concept pages carried this week's findings into the synthesis layer. Each is a standing page rather than a one-off, so the new evidence sits alongside what the page already argued.

### [Language learning](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/language-learning/)

The page now says plainly that evidence on chatbot speaking gains is uneven by feature, and that four of [the ten synthesised studies](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/chatbots-oral-proficiency-efl-systematic-review/) reported one holistic score, which is why their gains cannot be attributed to a feature. Read it before buying or building oral practice.

### [Intelligent tutoring](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/intelligent-tutoring/)

Its new finding is the retrieval result: [supplying reference strategies](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/tutor-case-bench-challenging-behaviors-2026/) lifts weak tutors and leaves strong ones where they were. That is a build decision, and the page separates it from the older claims about hint quality.

### [Self-report measures](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/self-report-measures/)

The sharpest new case of the self-report gap is intention, where a single score covered learning-oriented, shortcut-oriented, and mixed use in [the same sample of 340 students](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/high-school-genai-acceptance-tam-physics-2026/), with 68 re-administered items and eight interviews behind the claim.

### [Governance](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/concepts/governance/)

The page now leads with the [instrument-form finding](https://edtechdev.github.io/aied/pages/generative-ai-governance-education-scoping-review-2026/): 4 of 160 documents binding, 92 advisory, and accountability named as an actor in only 100. It reframes governance questions from what a policy says to whether it can act.
