**FPDS Apps and Resources** is the public hub for the teaching tools built and curated by Faculty Professional Development Services at Hillsborough College. One page gathers them under two headings, AI and accessibility, so that an instructor can find the office's own apps alongside the guides and assistants it recommends.

## What you can do with it

The AI section points to an interactive [[academic-integrity]] worksheet for redesigning assignments in the AI era, a self-paced tour of AI tools and teaching tasks, the *AI in Education Knowledge Base*, and a custom Gemini Gem for designing online modules. The accessibility section collects a Canvas checklist that begins with what you are working with and ends with a human check, a guide to [[universal-design-for-learning]] published under a non-commercial Creative Commons license, and a second Gemini Gem that writes alt text for images. Each entry is a working page rather than an announcement, and both sections link onward to material the office does not host.

## Who it is for

The site serves [[educational-development]] staff and instructors at Hillsborough College. It is also useful to instructors elsewhere as a worked example of a small faculty-development site, since it is a set of static pages that can be copied rather than rebuilt.

## Notes and caveats

Reading the site needs no account. The Gemini Gems and the NotebookLM notebook it links to are Google services, and those do need one. The repository is MIT-licensed as software, while the external guides it links to carry their own licenses. The office maintains the pages without a publication schedule, so they change without notice.
