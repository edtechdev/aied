> **Synthesis:** This example article page shows the structure and conventions used throughout the knowledge base. The blockquote summary runs 60-120 words (3-5 sentences) and states what the paper did, how it was done, and what it found. Article pages cover individual papers; broader topics that synthesize several papers belong on concept pages. The concepts a page touches are named in the typed frontmatter fields above — each one a real concept slug filed under that field's own registry section.

## Key Findings

- **Finding one** — the paper's main result, with the number the paper reports.
- **Finding two** — a second result, kept separate from the first.
- **Finding three** — a boundary condition, sample, or method detail that qualifies the first two.
- **Finding four** — what the study could not establish, and why.
- **Finding five** — the finding a practitioner would act on.

## What the study did

Two to four sections named for this paper's content rather than a template. Keep the whole body to
roughly 600-900 words and leave the detail in `raw/papers/<slug>.md`.

## What this means for practice

- **Instructors.** One action, imperative and derived from a finding on this page.
- **Designers.** Add other audience labels only when the paper supports a genuinely distinct
  implication for them, matched to the page's `audience:` field.

## Limitations

- Two to four bullets, each tied to a concrete fact about this study. Omit the section entirely
  rather than writing boilerplate such as "small sample, single institution".
