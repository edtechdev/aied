> **Synthesis:** This qualitative case study examines undergraduate students' engagement with GenAI in academic learning using self-determination theory and epistemic network analysis. Data from 23 semi-structured interviews revealed that students frequently described GenAI as supporting efficiency and conceptual understanding, yet persistent tensions concerning creativity, trust, and academic integrity were systematically interconnected. The findings support a shift from prohibition-oriented responses to transparent institutional guidance, autonomy-supportive scaffolding of verification practices, and process-oriented assessment designs.

## Key Findings

This qualitative case study examines undergraduate students' engagement with GenAI in academic learning using self-determination theory and epistemic network analysis. Data from 23 semi-structured interviews revealed that students frequently described GenAI as supporting efficiency and conceptual understanding, yet persistent tensions concerning creativity, trust, and academic integrity were systematically interconnected. The findings support a shift from prohibition-oriented responses to transparent institutional guidance, autonomy-supportive scaffolding of verification practices, and process-oriented assessment designs.
