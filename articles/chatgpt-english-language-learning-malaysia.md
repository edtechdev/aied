> **Synthesis:** Annamalai and colleagues (2026) conducted a qualitative thematic analysis with 25 students from a Malaysian higher education institution to understand their [[motivation]] for using ChatGPT in [[language-learning|English language learning]]. Grounded in [[self-determination-theory|Self-Determination Theory]] (SDT), the results demonstrated that ChatGPT contributes positively to English language competence, social connections, and independent learning processes. ChatGPT emerged as a helpful resource for enhancing grammar, [[writing-education|writing]], and conversational tasks, supporting learners' autonomy, relatedness, and competence. The study suggests ChatGPT allows [[teacher-role|educators]] to prioritize [[critical-thinking|higher-order thinking]] abilities and intricate areas of language training.

## Key Findings

- ChatGPT contributes positively to English language competence, social connections, and independent learning processes for Malaysian university students.
- The tool emerged as a helpful resource for enhancing grammar, writing, and conversational tasks in English learning.
- ChatGPT supports learners' autonomy, relatedness, and competence—the three key components of Self-Determination Theory.
- By handling foundational language tasks, ChatGPT allows educators to prioritize higher-order thinking and intricate areas of language training.
