# Contaminated Collaboration: Measuring Gender Bias Transfer in LLM-Assisted Student Writing

**Ariyan Hossain, Kazi Kamruzzaman Rabbi, Farig Sadeque, S M Taiabul Haque** (2026). arXiv cs.CL

## Key Findings

Gender-biased LLM prompts transfer bias into student-written career essays (N=123). Bias transfer is asymmetric: agency is suppressed in female-target essays while male-target writing is unaffected. Calls for fairness-aware design in educational AI tools.

## Relevance to AI in Education

This paper contributes directly to understanding how AI systems interact with learners in authentic educational settings.    Provides causal evidence that gender-biased LLM prompts transfer bias into student writing, with asymmetric effects suppressing female agency.
