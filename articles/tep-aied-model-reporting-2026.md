> **Synthesis:** Hwang, Xie, Wah, and Gasevic (2026) propose the TEP (Transparency-Ethics-[[pedagogy]])-AIED model, a streamlined three-dimensional framework for designing and reporting [[ai-education|AI in education]] research. The model addresses the fragmentation of existing reporting frameworks by integrating transparency (clear disclosure of AI system characteristics and learner/teacher-[[student-ai-interaction|AI interaction]] processes), ethics (data handling, risk mitigation, [[equity-in-ai-education|equity]], and [[agency|learner agency]]), and pedagogy (explicit articulation of learning objectives, theoretical alignment, and learner preparation). By positioning these dimensions as interconnected rather than isolated requirements, the TEP-AIED model supports methodological rigor while remaining accessible to researchers and practitioners.

## Key Findings

- The TEP-AIED model integrates three interconnected dimensions — Transparency, Ethics, and Pedagogy — to guide the design and reporting of AIED studies.
- Transparency requires clear disclosure of AI system characteristics and learner/teacher-AI interaction processes to strengthen interpretability and reproducibility.
- Ethics encourages proactive [[governance]] addressing data handling, risk mitigation, equity, and learner agency.
- Pedagogy requires explicit articulation of learning objectives, theoretical alignment, and learner preparation to develop appropriate conceptions of AI-supported learning and avoid uncritical reliance.
- The model is intended as practical guidance for reporting AIED research, embedding transparency, ethics, and pedagogy throughout the research lifecycle.

## Implications for AI in Education

The TEP-AIED model addresses a core challenge in [[research-methods-aied]]: existing frameworks for reporting AIED research are often complex, fragmented, or difficult to operationalize. By offering a streamlined three-dimensional framework, it strengthens validity, accountability, and educational relevance in AI-enhanced learning research. The model's emphasis on learner preparation and avoiding uncritical reliance connects to [[ai-literacy]] and [[cognitive-offloading|over-reliance]] concerns, while its ethical dimension aligns with [[ethics]] and [[ai-ed-evaluation]]. As a methodological contribution, it advances [[theory-development-aied]] by providing a practical reporting standard for the field.
