## Key Findings

Design for Assembly/Disassembly interventions improved open social robot usability from SUS 59.4 (Poor) to 89.4 (Excellent). Perceived workload predicts student uptake better than completion time. Co-creation with students in Guyana and Estonia.

## Relevance to AI in Education

This paper contributes directly to understanding how AI systems interact with learners in authentic educational settings.      Shows that co-created open social robots with DfA/DfD design achieve excellent usability and that perceived workload predicts student uptake better than build time.
