> **Synthesis:** Maurer, Brückner, Thyssen, Becker-Genschow, and Huwer (2026) investigate the need for AI-related post-qualification programs for in-service science educators. They examined the extent to which 115 in-service science educators in secondary education possess general AI knowledge (AI literacy) and how, why, and for what purposes they use AI in lesson planning, follow-up, and teaching. The findings identify relevant areas of focus for AI-related post-qualification programs, informing how science educators can be supported to use AI for lesson planning, implementation, and follow-up while preparing students for an AI-driven world.

## Key Findings

**In-service science educator focus.** The study examines AI literacy and AI usage among 115 in-service secondary science educators in Germany.

**Uses of AI.** Educators use AI to support lesson planning, implementation, and follow-up, as well as in teaching itself.

**AI knowledge and literacy.** The study measures general AI knowledge (AI literacy) and how it relates to actual AI use.

**Post-qualification need.** Findings identify what AI-related post-qualification programs should focus on to help in-service science educators leverage AI effectively.

**Implication.** Professional development for in-service science educators should build both AI literacy and practical, pedagogical AI use, linking to the wiki's [[stem-education]], [[ai-literacy]], and [[teacher-education]] concepts.
