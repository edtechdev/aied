> **Synthesis:** Ó Ceallaigh and Murphy (2026) systematically review the GenAI-specific Technological Pedagogical Knowledge (TPK) required by teacher educators (TEs) who prepare post-primary teachers — a critical but underexplored area. TPK for GenAI extends beyond digital competence to encompass pedagogical reasoning, ethical awareness, and AI-augmented instructional design. Reviewing 17 peer-reviewed studies focused on teacher educators' engagement with GenAI, the review highlights persistent challenges in developing GenAI-related TPK, particularly around ethical concerns (data privacy, algorithmic bias, authorship, accountability) in teacher preparation within higher education institutions.

## Key Findings

**Underexplored teacher-educator TPK.** The review addresses the GenAI-specific TPK required by teacher educators, a critical gap in the literature on preparing teachers who will themselves teach with AI.

**TPK extends beyond digital competence.** GenAI-related TPK encompasses pedagogical reasoning, ethical awareness, and AI-augmented instructional design, not just technical proficiency.

**Seventeen studies.** A systematic review of 17 peer-reviewed studies focuses on teacher educators' engagement with GenAI across major academic databases.

**Persistent challenges.** Developing GenAI-related TPK faces persistent difficulties, particularly around data privacy, algorithmic bias, authorship, and accountability — salient in teacher preparation within higher-education institutions.

**Implication.** Teacher-education programs must explicitly build teacher educators' GenAI-TPK, connecting to the wiki's [[teacher-education]] and [[tpack]] concepts.
