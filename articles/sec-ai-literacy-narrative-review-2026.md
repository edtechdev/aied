> **Synthesis:** Palmquist, Sigurdardottir, and Myhre (2025) conduct a narrative literature review examining the intersection of AI literacy and social-emotional competencies (SEC) in education. The review identifies three themes through thematic analysis: AI's transformative potential in reshaping educational practices, its influence on educational providers and stakeholders, and the ethical considerations of AI integration. The authors propose an integrated framework showing that combining AI literacy with social-emotional competencies enhances educational outcomes by promoting personalized learning, fostering collaboration, and addressing ethical challenges.

## Key Findings

- Narrative review identifies three themes: AI's transformative potential, its influence on providers/stakeholders, and ethical considerations of AI integration
- Argues AI literacy and social-emotional competencies are complementary — technical proficiency alone is insufficient for navigating AI-mediated learning environments
- The integrated framework supports a holistic approach where digital and relational competencies are equally valued
- Positions educators as needing both technological skill and emotional intelligence to prepare students for AI-rich futures
