> **Synthesis:** Feng and Huang (2026) proposed and validated a [[personalized-learning|personalized learning]] path optimization framework that integrates Bayesian cognitive diagnosis, knowledge space theory, and cognitive load theory to address the sparsity of large-scale educational data and the lack of empirical testing of the psychological mechanism behind personalized paths. In the first phase, a Bayesian DINA model was trained on the EdNet public dataset (N=5,000) with a shortest remediation path algorithm; in the second phase, a dataset of 120 students validated the algorithm's effectiveness. The framework demonstrates how Bayesian [[cognitive-diagnosis]] can optimize [[adaptive-learning|adaptive learning]] paths while making the underlying [[cognitive-offloading|cognitive load]] mechanism empirically testable.

## Key Findings

- The framework integrates Bayesian cognitive diagnosis, knowledge space theory, and cognitive load theory to optimize personalized learning paths.
- A Bayesian DINA model was trained on the EdNet public dataset (N=5,000) and paired with a shortest remediation path algorithm.
- A second-phase dataset of 120 students validated the algorithm's effectiveness in guiding personalized remediation.
- The study addresses both the sparsity convergence problem of traditional DINA models and the untested psychological mechanism (cognitive load) underlying personalized path effectiveness.
