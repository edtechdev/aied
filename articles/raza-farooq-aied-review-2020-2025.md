> **Synthesis:** Raza & Farooq (2025) conduct a comprehensive content analysis of AI in education from 2020-2025, examining 100+ peer-reviewed articles through a three-layer framework: the genome layer (infrastructure, algorithms), the cognitive layer (predictive analytics, multimodal sensing, discourse analysis), and the symbiotic layer (learning platforms, smart classrooms, GenAI copilots). Three forward trends identified: human-AI co-orchestration, privacy-preserving edge/federated AI, and authentic multimodal assessment.

## Three-Layer Framework

This review organizes AIED research into three interrelated layers:

- **Genome Layer:** The foundational elements — instructional customs, evaluation practices, digital tools, and algorithmic schemas that underpin AI in education.
- **Cognitive Layer:** Instructional mechanisms including [[personalized-learning|personalized learning]] via measure-model-adapt loops, predictive analytics, [[multimodal]] sensing, discourse and affect analysis.
- **Symbiotic Layer:** End-to-end implementations including [[edtech-platform|learning platforms]], smart classrooms, automation of processes, and [[generative-ai|generative AI]] copilot systems.

## Key Findings

- **100+ peer-reviewed articles** examined, covering empirical and analytical studies
- **Three forward trends:** (1) human-AI co-orchestration as default classroom pattern; (2) privacy-preserving, edge/federated AI; (3) authentic multimodal assessment via [[generative-ai]] simulations
- **Effects summarized** on learning outcomes, engagement, teacher workload, and institutional adoption
- **Actionable guidance** for researchers, practitioners, and system leaders

## Implications

This review provides a structured map of the AIED landscape during the LLM era (2020-2025), useful as a reference point for [[ai-education]]. The three-layer framework connects to [[learning-analytics]], [[intelligent-tutoring]], and [[adaptive-learning]] research, while the forward trends align with discussions in [[governance]] and institutional AI readiness.
