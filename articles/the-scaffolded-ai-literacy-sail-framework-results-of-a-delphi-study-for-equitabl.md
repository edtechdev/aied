> **Synthesis:** This article reports on a Delphi study that created the Scaffolded AI Literacy (SAIL) framework, broadly applicable across contexts while accessible enough for curriculum assimilation. Unlike many existing frameworks, SAIL provides a scaffolded pathway through competency levels applicable across all ages and stages of education, helping address second- and third-level digital divides. The article details the Delphi study process, key decisions, the resulting framework, and its contribution to equitable access to AI literacies.
