> **Synthesis:** Passey et al. (2026b) apply the social justice-oriented digital transformation framework to four case studies of community-led design and implementation of digital technology with marginalized populations. Grounded in a vision of social justice and the principles of equity, inclusivity, participation, empowerment and ethical responsibility, the framework's five pillars — accessibility and infrastructure, inclusive pedagogy and curriculum, capacity building, ethical governance, and monitoring and evaluation — are shown to apply across the four cases. The analysis demonstrates the framework's viability both for analysing previously implemented digital-transformation experiences and for guiding new initiatives through an educational social-justice perspective, complementing the framework-development paper.

## Key Findings

**Four community-led cases.** The framework is applied to four case studies of community-led design and implementation of digital technology with marginalized populations, testing its real-world viability.

**Framework application.** Through the five pillars — accessibility and infrastructure, inclusive pedagogy and curriculum, capacity building, ethical governance, and monitoring and evaluation — the analysis shows how the social-justice framework applies to each case.

**Dual utility.** The framework proves relevant both for analysing previously implemented digital-transformation experiences and for guiding new initiatives from an educational social-justice perspective — bridging retrospective analysis and prospective design.

**Normative grounding.** The cases are examined through principles of equity, inclusivity, participation, empowerment and ethical responsibility, embedding [[equity-in-ai-education|equity]] and [[ethics]] at the core of digital-transformation assessment.

**Companion to framework paper.** This application study complements the framework-development paper, together providing conceptual foundation plus empirical illustration of social justice-oriented digital transformation in education.
