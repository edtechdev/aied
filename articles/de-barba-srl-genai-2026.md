> **[[agency|Learner agency]] across scales** — a theoretical/integrative paper proposing an account of learner agency that extends [[self-regulated-learning|SRL]] beyond task-focused [[regulation]] by connecting it with complex systems science and Freirean [[critical-pedagogy|critical pedagogy]]. It foregrounds three dimensions of learner agency — **regulation, integration, and positioning** — and a cross-scale [[scaffolding|scaffold]] supporting learners to move between them in algorithmically mediated environments.

## Key Findings

- **Three dimensions of learner agency.** *Regulation* (the self as an active managing process, drawing on social-cognitive SRL); *Integration* (the self as an organising centre across time and contexts, drawing on complex systems science); *Positioning* (the self as a critical interpreter, drawing on Freirean critical [[pedagogy]]). Agency lies in the coordination/movement between these, not any one alone.
- **Two limitations of SRL.** SRL has progressively narrowed to task-focused regulation (leaving broader life conditions and structural positions under-theorised), and its operationalisation in educational technology has narrowed the construct to optimisable behavioural proxies (e.g., time management, strategy use, effort allocation).
- **Cross-scale scaffold.** A design orientation with four functions — surfacing patterns across scales, opening conditions to critique, returning interpretation to action, and centring the learner as the agent of connection. It extends SRL from "agency within conditions" to "agency over conditions" (questioning what metrics measure and whose purposes they encode).
- **Critical stance on [[learning-analytics]] and [[generative-ai]].** Algorithmically mediated environments encode assumptions about what counts as [[student-engagement|engagement]] and who succeeds; supporting agency requires learners to interpret their data, question what it does not capture, and act on that interpretation — a critique that has been largely disconnected from SRL theory.

## Implications

- **Reconnect SRL with critical pedagogy and learning analytics.** The integrative perspective offers a theoretical foundation for educational technology that places human agency at the centre — learners as agents who understand and shape the conditions of their development, not merely responsive to systems.
- **Design for cross-scale movement.** Rather than optimising behaviour at a single scale, educational AI should support learners moving between immediate-task regulation, trajectory-wide integration, and critical positioning.
