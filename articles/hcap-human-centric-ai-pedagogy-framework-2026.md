> **Synthesis:** Chiu (2026) proposes the Human-Centric AI Pedagogy (HCAP) framework, an evolution of TPACK designed for the generative AI era. Arguing that AI's agentic autonomy, epistemic complexities, and ethical dimensions render the established Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge model insufficient, the framework integrates five knowledge domains: AI-Technological, AI-Content, AI-Pedagogical, Human-AI Collaborative, and Ethical Knowledge. A three-round Delphi study with 30 teachers across diverse subjects produced consensus on 25 critical knowledge items, providing an empirically grounded model that translates theoretical AI pedagogy into actionable teacher competencies.

## Key Findings

- HCAP extends TPACK with two new knowledge domains — Human-AI Collaborative Knowledge and Ethical Knowledge — responding to AI's agentic autonomy and ethical complexity
- A three-round Delphi method with 30 teachers from diverse subjects refined a consensus list of 25 critical knowledge items across the five HCAP domains
- The framework provides a concrete roadmap for teacher education, translating theory into actionable competencies for orchestrating human-AI collaborative learning
- Positions teachers as strategic orchestrators who use AI ethically, critically, and productively rather than merely as tool users
