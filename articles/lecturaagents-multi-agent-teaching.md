## Key Findings

Hierarchical multi-agent framework (ProfessorAgent + subordinate agents) enables end-to-end adaptive embodied teaching. TASA algorithm aligns teaching actions with learner profiles. Outperforms baselines on lecture quality, embodiment, assessment, and personalization.

## Relevance to AI in Education

This paper contributes directly to understanding how AI systems interact with learners in authentic educational settings.   Introduces hierarchical multi-agent architectures for embodied, personalized teaching that adapts lecture content and actions to individual learners.
