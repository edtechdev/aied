> **Synthesis:** Xie and Luo (2026) develop and validate a domain-specific AI-TPACK instrument tailored to mathematics teacher education, then survey 412 Chinese mathematics teacher education students (MTES). Their AI-TPACK readiness is currently at a preliminary stage, with no significant differences across grade levels. A structural equation model shows that while self-efficacy is a significant positive predictor of AI-TPACK, strong traditional teaching beliefs can paradoxically act as a cognitive barrier to AI integration. The findings provide empirical evidence for redesigning mathematics teacher training to address both technical proficiency and psychological readiness.

## Key Findings

**Domain-specific instrument.** The study developed and validated an AI-TPACK instrument tailored to the unique pedagogical and logical demands of mathematics education.

**Preliminary readiness.** Survey of 412 Chinese MTES revealed AI-TPACK readiness is currently at a preliminary stage, with no significant grade-level differences across the teacher-education curriculum.

**Self-efficacy as predictor.** Self-efficacy serves as a significant positive predictor of AI-TPACK.

**Teaching beliefs as barrier.** Strong traditional teaching beliefs can paradoxically act as a cognitive barrier to AI integration.

**Implication.** Mathematics teacher training must be redesigned to address both technical proficiency and psychological readiness (self-efficacy and beliefs), linking to the wiki's [[teacher-education]] and [[math-education]] concepts.
