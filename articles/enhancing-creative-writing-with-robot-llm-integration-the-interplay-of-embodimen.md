> **Synthesis:** This study explores the impact of robot-LLM integration on collaborative creative writing, focusing on how embodiment and AI creativity influence creative output. With 150 undergraduate students across five conditions, results revealed that the Human-Robot (High-Creativity LLM) condition significantly enhanced originality, while Human-Human and Human-LLM collaborations excelled in imagery and voice. An 'embodiment paradox' was identified, where robot embodiment amplified creativity in high-creativity AI conditions but human collaboration remained superior in stylistic expression.

## Key Findings
