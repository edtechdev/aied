> **Synthesis:** Chiu, Bali, Tondeur, Howard, and Chan (2026) apply self-determination theory (SDT) to investigate how need-supportive professional development impacts teachers' AI literacy, attitudes, anxiety, and engagement in online professional learning communities. Using a sequential mixed-methods approach with 382 secondary school teachers, the study found that need-supportive professional development enhances AI literacy and fosters sustained behavioral engagement in professional learning communities. Qualitative analysis identified nine specific design strategies for professional development programs, bridging the gap between isolated professional development and professional learning communities.

## Key Findings

- Need-supportive professional development grounded in self-determination theory enhances teachers' AI literacy and fosters sustained behavioral engagement in online professional learning communities
- Sequential mixed-methods study with 382 secondary school teachers; quantitative results confirmed AI literacy gains and engagement
- Qualitative analysis identified nine specific design strategies for professional development supporting autonomy, competence, and relatedness
- Addresses the critical but overlooked connection between professional development and professional learning communities for scaling and sustaining teacher AI learning
