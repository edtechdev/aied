> **Synthesis:** Laferrière and Cox (2026) analyse sixteen years of EDUsummIT official reports and E-Books (2009–2025) to map how information technology and, more recently, AI have been used in primary and secondary education. Using NotebookLM as an interactive Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) mediating artifact, they run a three-phase analysis — systematic, thematic-pattern, and Cultural-Historical Activity Theory (CHAT) — revealing persisting integration issues and systemic misalignments. Contradictions among the activity systems of researchers, practitioners and policymakers explain both continuities and shifts in EDUsummIT's priority objects over the period. The paper closes with four guiding shifts toward an evidence-based framework for future IT and AI integration, and demonstrates the value of RAG-based analysis for longitudinal corpora.

## Key Findings

**Sixteen-year corpus.** Analysis spans EDUsummIT official reports and E-Books from 2009 to 2025, capturing how IT and (later) AI have been used across [[k-12]] education.

**RAG-mediated analysis.** NotebookLM served as an interactive Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) artifact for the three-phase analysis — systematic, thematic-pattern, and CHAT — illustrating a novel methodological use of [[rag]] for longitudinal document corpora.

**Persisting misalignments.** Integration issues and systemic contradictions recur across activity systems: misaligned objects, rules and divisions of labour among researchers, practitioners and policymakers constrain classroom innovation — a structural diagnosis relevant to [[educational-policy-ai]].

**Explaining priority shifts.** The identified contradictions account for continuities and shifts in EDUsummIT priority objects across the sixteen years, linking policy priorities to underlying systemic tensions.

**Four guiding shifts.** The analysis concludes with four shifts toward an evidence-based framework for future IT and AI integration in education, plus evidence that RAG analysis benefits similar longitudinal investigations.
