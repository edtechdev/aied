> **Synthesis:** This study introduces a Digital Interactions Quality (DigIQ) framework and scale as a protocol to observe and index the quality of interactions and experiences involving digital technologies with children in Early Childhood Education and Care (ECEC) settings. Observations in 50 pre-school classrooms validated the framework, revealing that quality ratings were higher when learning intentions drove activity and digital technologies enhanced or transformed the learning experience rather than merely replicating non-digital activities.

## Key Findings

This study introduces a Digital Interactions Quality (DigIQ) framework and scale as a protocol to observe and index the quality of interactions and experiences involving digital technologies with children in Early Childhood Education and Care (ECEC) settings. Observations in 50 pre-school classrooms validated the framework, revealing that quality ratings were higher when learning intentions drove activity and digital technologies enhanced or transformed the learning experience rather than merely replicating non-digital activities.
