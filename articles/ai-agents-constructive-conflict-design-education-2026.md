> **Synthesis:** Investigates adversarial AI design agents that enact constructive conflict to prompt reconsideration in novice designers. Between-subjects experiment (N=48) comparing adversarial vs. cooperative AI agent roles. Adversarial agent condition produced significantly more design iterations, broader exploration of alternatives, and higher-rated final designs. Participants reported the conflict agent was frustrating but ultimately helpful — a productive friction dynamic. Connects to adversarial design theory and Socratic tutoring.
