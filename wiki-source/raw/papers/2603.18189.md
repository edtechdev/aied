## Title
TeachingCoach: A Fine-Tuned Scaffolding Chatbot for Instructional Guidance to Instructors

## Authors
Isabel Molnar, Peiyu Li, Si Chen, Sugana Chawla, James Lang, Ronald Metoyer, Ting Hua, Nitesh V. Chawla

## Abstract / Summary
Pedagogically grounded chatbot for instructor PD. Data-centric pipeline extracts pedagogical rules + synthetic dialogue fine-tuning. Outperforms GPT-4o mini on clarity/reflectiveness/responsiveness.
