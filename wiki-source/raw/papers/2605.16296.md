# Artificial Intelligence in Lifelong Learning: Opportunities and Challenges in Adult Education Policy

**Authors:** Andresa Theodora, Nikolaos Tselios
**Published:** 2026-04-15
**Venue:** arXiv:2605.16296 [cs.CY]

## Abstract
Examines AI's role in adult education policy, covering personalized learning, intelligent tutoring, learning analytics, and workforce development. Highlights concerns: digital divide, data privacy, algorithmic bias, over-reliance, and institutional readiness. Argues AI in lifelong learning must be approached as a socio-technical and ethical issue requiring careful governance.
