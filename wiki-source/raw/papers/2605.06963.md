# From Surface Learning to Deep Understanding: A Grounded AI Tutoring System for Moodle

**Authors:** Anna Ostrowska, Michał Kukla, Gabriela Majstrak
**Published:** 2026-05-07
**arXiv:** [2605.06963](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06963)

## Abstract

Presents a grounded AI tutoring system integrated into the Moodle LMS, designed to move students from surface-level learning to deep conceptual understanding through adaptive questioning and feedback.
