# Towards Self-Referential Analytic Assessment: A Profile-Based Approach to L2 Writing Evaluation with LLMs

**Authors:** Stefano Bannò, Kate Knill, Mark Gales
**Published:** 2026-05-05
**arXiv:** [2605.04298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04298)

## Abstract

Proposes a self-referential assessment framework for L2 writing evaluation that identifies intra-learner strengths and weaknesses rather than inter-learner rankings, finding LLMs outperform single human raters at identifying relative weaknesses.
