# MBP-KT: Learning Global Collaborative Information from Meta-Behavioral Pattern for Enhanced Knowledge Tracing
arXiv: 2605.08697
Authors: Jia, Y., Li, D., Chen, J., Mao, Z., Tong, M., Li, Y., Wang, X.
Published: 2026-05-09
Categories: cs.AI
Venue: arXiv preprint

## Abstract
MBP-KT transforms raw learner interactions into meta-behavioral patterns, extracts global collaborative representations without additional parameters, and provides universal injection strategies for any downstream KT architecture.
