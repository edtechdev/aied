## New Ingested Papers

### 1. Cross-Subject Predictive Validity for Delayed Start Behavior
**[[cross-subject-validity-delayed-start]]** — Gutterman, Gurung, Branstetter, Koedinger & Aleven (2026)
Delayed start behavior (students starting work past the recommended time) predicts learning outcomes across different subjects, showing cross-subject predictive validity as a generalizable behavioral detector. Reduces the need for per-course calibration of at-risk student models.

### 2. AI Coaching for Accelerating Human Skill Development with RL
**[[ai-coaching-rl-skill-development]]** — Wang, Gu, Loquercio, Hu & Mangharam (2026)
An RL-based AI coach that dynamically balances guidance with learner autonomy accelerates motor-skill development. Excessive intervention leads to over-reliance and skill atrophy, consistent with scaffolding theory in intelligent tutoring.
