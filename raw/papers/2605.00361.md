# Pedagogical Promise and Peril of AI: A Text Mining Analysis of ChatGPT Research Discussions in Programming Education
arXiv: 2605.00361
Authors: Grume, J.C., Miranda, J.P.P., De Leon, A.P., Salenga, J.L., Hernandez, H.E., Castro, M.A.A., Maniago, V.G.M., Canlas, J.D., Quiambao, J.B.
Published: 2026-05-01
Categories: cs.CY, cs.AI
Venue: Pedagogical Innovations in CS Education (IGI Global)

## Abstract
Text mining analysis mapping how literature frames ChatGPT in programming education across four themes: pedagogical implementation, student-centered learning, AI infrastructure, and assessment design. Dual positioning: promise (feedback, efficiency) versus peril (overreliance, integrity).
