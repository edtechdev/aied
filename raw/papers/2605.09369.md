# Explainable Knowledge Tracing via Probabilistic Embeddings and Pattern-based Reasoning
arXiv: 2605.09369
Authors: Wu, S., Xu, C., Zhang, W.
Published: 2026-05-10
Categories: cs.AI
Venue: arXiv preprint

## Abstract
PLKT introduces probabilistic logical knowledge tracing that uses beta-distributed embeddings and transparent logical operations to construct auditable reasoning paths linking historical student actions to predictions.
