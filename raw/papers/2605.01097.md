# Interpretable Difficulty-Aware Knowledge Tracing in Tutor-Student Dialogues
arXiv: 2605.01097
Authors: Huang, S., Scarlatos, A., Lee, J., Lan, A.
Published: 2026-05-01
Categories: cs.CL, cs.AI
Venue: arXiv preprint

## Abstract
Existing dialogue-based knowledge tracing (KT) approaches ignore question difficulty modeling and rely on opaque latent representations. We propose an interpretable, difficulty-aware conversational KT framework that integrates Item Response Theory to map LLM outputs into student ability and question difficulty parameters.
