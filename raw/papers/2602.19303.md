## Title
The Path to Conversational AI Tutors: Integrating Tutoring Best Practices and Targeted Technologies to Produce Scalable AI Agents

## Authors
Kirk Vanacore, Ryan S. Baker, Avery H. Closser, Jeremy Roschelle

## Abstract / Summary
Synthesis paper bridging ITS research + GenAI. Keep/Change/Center/Study framework. Keep knowledge tracing/affect detection; Change delivery with GenAI; Center meaning-making/student agency; Study efficacy/integration.
