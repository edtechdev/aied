Our response (and definition). We agree that tutoring
involves affect-accuracy trade-offs and that tact, hedging,
and deference to course conventions can be appropriate.
Our claim is narrower. We do not label a response syco-
phantic merely because it is warm, cautious, or deferential.
The failure occurs when the tutor validates or substantially
blurs a misconception that it should correct in the default
instructional frame, and does so specifically after agreement-
seeking pressure. Under this definition, educational safety
concerns whether a deployed tutor avoids increasing durable
