# Balancing Teacher and Student Agency: Co-Orchestration Tool Design Supporting Real-Time Dynamic Pairing

**Authors:** Kexin Bella Yang, Menghan Liu, Liyi Xu, Nikol Rummel, Vincent Aleven
**Published:** 2026-05-19
**Venue:** arXiv:2605.18761 [cs.HC] — Accepted at CSCW 2026, to appear in PACM HCI

## Abstract
Explores the design space of distributing control between teachers and students during dynamic pairing transitions between individual practice and collaborative problem-solving. Uses participatory speed dating and mixed-method analysis across three pairing stages (before, during, after). Recommends structured teacher guidance initially with progressively increasing student autonomy.
