EMNLP 2025 Demo. Emotionally aware LLM-powered math tutor. Dynamically models student emotions from conversational text and facial expressions, aggregated from both modalities to prompt emotionally-aware LLM responses. Maps affective states to relevant pedagogical strategies. Win rate: +23 points. DAMR score: +3 points at overall level. Evaluated across eight pedagogical dimensions plus user studies.
