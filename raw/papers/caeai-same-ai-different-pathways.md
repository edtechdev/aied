# Same AI, different pathways: Unpacking mechanisms of AI-mediated learning across discipline-institution contexts

**Authors:** Qinjie Shen
**Source:** Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, Vol 11, 2026
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100601
**License:** CC BY 4.0 (Open Access)

## Key Findings

Mixed-methods study of Design (n=221) and Business (n=222) students in Thailand. AI literacy predicted prompting proficiency and verification behavior (p<.001). Two mechanisms: prompting→trust calibration and verification→reduced cognitive load. Context differences: Design students used ChatGPT for ideation/refinement; Business students for structured analysis/auditability. Verification functioned as metacognitive safeguard.
