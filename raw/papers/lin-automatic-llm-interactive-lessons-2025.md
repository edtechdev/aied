ECTEL 2025. GPT-4o with RAG generating interactive scenario-based lessons for novice math tutors. Topics: Encouraging Students' Independence, Encouraging Help-Seeking Behavior, Turning on Cameras. Task decomposition prompting (breaking lesson generation into sub-tasks) outperformed single-step generation. Evaluated by two human evaluators using comprehensive rubric. Strengths: well-structured content, time-saving. Limitations: generic feedback, lack of clarity in some instructional sections. Supports hybrid human-AI approaches for tutor training content creation.
