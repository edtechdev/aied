# Students' multimodal prompting practices as epistemic work in AI literacy development

**Authors:** Sylvana Sofkova Hashemi
**Source:** Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, Vol 11, 100635
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100635
**License:** CC BY 4.0 (Open Access)

## Key Findings

Empirical study with 28 postgraduate students engaged in collaborative multimodal prompting tasks. Found prompting strategies vary along a continuum from basic input-output to strategic, iterative, dialogic practices. Prompting emerges as a central epistemic practice — students critically interpret, refine, and negotiate AI outputs. Multimodal engagement exposes challenges in translating abstract meaning into machine-readable prompts. Ethical dimensions of AI literacy remain underdeveloped despite gains in evaluation and creation.
