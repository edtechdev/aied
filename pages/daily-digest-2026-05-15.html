May 14, 2026 Submissions

1. eduagentbench-agent-teaching-benchmark — Chen et al. EduAgentBench: first holistic benchmark for AI tutor agents spanning pedagogical judgment, situated tutoring, and LMS workflows. 150 tasks. Frontier models show bounded judgment but fail at autonomous teaching.

2. agentic-ai-ecosystems-higher-education — Sudarshan et al. 50-page perspective on integrated multi-agent AI for higher education. Identifies fragmentation, lack of cross-functional integration, and insufficient inclusivity.

3. computational-thinking-ai-agent-creation — Sun et al. Mixed-methods study (n=93) of no-code AI agent creation for K-12 CT. Optimal Development Zone effect (η²=0.55): moderate-CT students gain most.

4. international-students-conversational-ai-adaptation — Nourian et al. Survey+interview study: international students use AI as short-term first-aid but want long-term support companions.

5. eduframetrap-llm-sycophancy-educational-safety — Kasneci & Kasneci Reasoning-Sycophancy Paradox: LLMs resist context-switch attacks but capitulate under authority/social pressure. EduFrameTrap benchmark introduced.

Earlier May 2026 Submissions

6. taklif-ai-interest-based-personalized-assignments — Kurdya et al. (May 7) Interest-based assignment generation with Llama 3.3 70B. 84% user approval.

7. lata-ferpa-compliant-local-llm-autograder — Rodríguez (May 6) Local, FERPA-compliant autograder. +11% midterm, +8% final, p<10⁻²⁷ confidence gains.

8. prompt-injection-defenses-educational-llm-tutors — Maiorano (Mar 29, arXiv:2605) Security-usability-latency trade-offs in educational LLM guardrails. Zero-FPR pipeline.

9. students-llm-usage-critical-thinking — Park et al. (May 6, EDM'26) LLM usage categorization in critical thinking; student initiative predicts learning.

10. learnmate2-llm-adaptive-learning — Wang et al. (May 7) LLM-powered adaptive learning system; beats baseline platform+LLM.
