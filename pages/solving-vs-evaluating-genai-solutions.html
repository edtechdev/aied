Is Solving Better Than Evaluating GenAI Solutions?

Created: 2026-07-31 | Tags: generative-aistem-educationhigher-edefficacy-studystudent-experience

Summary

Randomized A/B crossover study (N=22) in upper-division computing courses comparing solution evaluation/critique tasks against traditional solution generation. Finds that evaluation-centered tasks produce comparable or better learning outcomes, suggesting an effective pedagogical response to the availability of GenAI code generation tools.

The work connects to broader discussions in AI and education around generative-ai, computing-education, student-experience, contributing to our understanding of how generative ai shapes educational practice.
