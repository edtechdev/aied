1. GenAI Impact on Chinese HSS Students

genai-impact-chinese-students-hss — Fan & Liu (2026) A large-scale survey of Chinese humanities and social sciences students found over half reported enhanced learning motivation, independent thinking, and creativity from GenAI use, though a substantial minority saw little change. Academic performance gains were widely reported, but these may partly reflect assessment limitations. Accuracy concerns and over-reliance emerged as top challenges.

2. SupplyNet: Visual Exploratory Learning

supplynet-visual-exploratory-learning — Li, Han, Hu, Cao, Qu & Song (2026) SupplyNet uses a contextual graph-based LLM multi-agent framework for supply chain management education, combining interactive network visualization, branching timeline for "what-if" exploration, and task-oriented analysis. A user study found increased engagement and improved perceived understanding.
