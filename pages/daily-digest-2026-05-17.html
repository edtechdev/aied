ai-tpack-teacher-multi-agent-workflow — ◐

Sun et al. (2026) identify three teacher archetypes (Systematic Optimizers, Prolific Creators, Passive Observers) in multi-agent instructional workflow design. AI-TPACK integration emerges from the dynamic interplay of systems thinking, pedagogical beliefs, and self-efficacy — not from possessing discrete knowledge domains. Calls for differentiated scaffolding responsive to teacher cognitive-behavioral diversity.

affective-text-wearable-student-health — ◐

Harry et al. (2026) demonstrate in a year-long study of 458 students that ultra-brief affective text (median 3 words) enriches wearable physiological data. Emotional register, not topical content, carries the predictive signal. General pretrained NLP outperforms domain-adapted models for health outcomes.

codify-socratic-tutoring-programming — ◐

Ilyasov et al. (2026) present Codify, an LLM-powered Socratic tutoring platform for programming education featuring RAG, competency tracking, adaptive question generation, code analysis, and gamification. Deployed via AWS Bedrock at St. John's University with promising preliminary engagement results.

Key Themes

  • Affect over topic: How students express matters more than what they say for health prediction
  • Socratic + RAG: Convergent design pattern for LLM tutoring — discovery-based, not answer-giving
