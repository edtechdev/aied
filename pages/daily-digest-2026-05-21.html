New Papers Ingested (7)

code-review-genai-cs1 — Combating Harms of Generative AI in CS1 with Code Review Interviews and a Flipped Classroom

Fowles et al. (2026) — Utah State University. Three semesters of data show oral code review assessments + flipped classroom mitigate GenAI harms in CS1. Keystroke logs confirmed significantly higher AI usage, yet exam scores showed no decline. Students responded positively to the interview format.

genai-architecture-education — Gen-AI-tecture: using generative AI to support architectural students in design tasks

Kapsalis (2026) — Mixed-methods focus-group study. Locally executed, discipline-specific gen-AI tool enhanced creative fluency and broadened participation across diverse learner profiles. Students gained confidence in AI-supported design workflows.

ai-pedagogical-accompaniment-amico — Design Principles and Observable Indicators for AI-Enabled Pedagogical Accompaniment (Amico)

Benedetti (2026) — Accepted at ICAIE 2026. Dual-mode prototype (AmicoMio: structured; AmicoTuo: reflective) with five human-in-command design principles. Cross-context pilot in Italy and China showed feasibility in vocational education.

concept-catalyst-engineering-scaffolds — Creating Learning Scaffolds for Engineering Design Using Concept Catalyst

Singh, Mansi & Riedl (2026) — Georgia Tech. Accepted at ISLS 2026. LLM-powered tool helps K-12 teachers rapidly create scaffold questions for engineering design challenges. Three-stage pipeline: concept decomposition → visual manipulation → question generation with teacher modification.

teacher-student-agency-orchestration — Balancing Teacher and Student Agency: Co-Orchestration Tool Design Supporting Real-Time Dynamic Pairing

Yang et al. (2026) — CMU / UW / Ruhr-Universität Bochum. Accepted at CSCW 2026. Participatory speed dating study maps a three-stage design space for balancing teacher orchestration and student autonomy during dynamic pairing transitions. Recommends structured teacher guidance with progressively increasing student autonomy.

ai-lifelong-learning-policy — Artificial Intelligence in Lifelong Learning: Opportunities and Challenges in Adult Education Policy

Theodora & Tselios (2026) — Policy synthesis examining AI's dual role in adult education. Covers personalized learning, intelligent tutoring, learning analytics, and workforce development against challenges of digital divide, privacy, bias, and institutional readiness. Argues for socio-technical governance.

anvil-ai-educational-animations — ANVIL: Analogies and Videos for Lecturers

Noviello, Birillo & Migut (2026) — Delft University of Technology. End-to-end pipeline: analogy generation → screenplay → manim code with automated repair. Teacher evaluation found materials frequently rated adequate; educators responded positively to perceived value and usability.
