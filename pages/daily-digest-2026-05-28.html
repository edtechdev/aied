1. correct-answer-trap-ai-tutor (Imran & Bulathwela, AIED'26) — LLM tutors suffer from "Correct Answer Trap": 71% of missed misconceptions occur when flawed reasoning accidentally yields correct answers. Even frontier models produce 4 false alarms per true detection.

7. llm-sentiment-analysis-education-research (Gonzalez et al.) — LLM-assisted sentiment analysis workflow enables multi-variable mixed-methods comparison in education research.

8. post-covid-ict-career-aspirations (Popa et al.) — PISA 2018-2022 analysis: digital skills are strongest predictor of ICT career aspirations; teacher support plays complementary role.
