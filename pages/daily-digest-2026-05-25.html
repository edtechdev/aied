New Papers

cognitive-offloading-speedup-illusion — CogSci 2026

Yu, Cheng, Jabbar, Sucholutsky, Collins, Jurafsky, Hawkins — Stanford / Princeton N=1,237: Users overestimate AI time savings for simple tasks. Actual completion times identical between AI-assisted and independent work. Companion to efficiency-gain illusion (arXiv:2605.22687, already ingested).

ai-fatigue-academic-contexts

Miranda, Parreño, Rivera — Philippines universities Grounded theory with 1,054 students identifies 5 dimensions: Cognitive Overload, Motivational Disengagement, Moral Unease, Physical Strain, Attentional Drift. Stage-based AI Fatigue Model.

socially-fluent-ai-identity-detection

Yan, Jin, Han, Gašević — Monash / Tsinghua N=786: Humans fail to detect AI teammates above chance in text-based groups. Flawed suspicion heuristics (speed, fluency) used instead of actual behavioral signals.

mindcopilot-llm-co-writing — IJCAI 2026

Fang et al. — USTC / Shanghai AI Lab Formalizes co-writing as Human-in-the-Loop MDP. Co-Writing Fidelity Suite: Hierarchical Acceptance Rate + Knowledge-aware Editing Distance.

neurodivergent-computing-students

Zastudil, Muthusekaran, Nasimova, MacNeil — Temple University Survey (n=24 ND, 20 NT) + interviews: neurodivergent computing students prefer small teams with defined roles. Unstructured collaborative assignments cause discomfort.

stanbkt-bayesian-knowledge-tracing

Pradhan, Pei, Lee, Zhang, Ottmar, Sales — WPI Open-source Python package for Bayesian Knowledge Tracing in Stan. 4 estimation methods, 3 model variants, uncertainty quantification for ITS parameter comparison.
