cognitive-shift-ai-education — Evidence of a Cognitive Shift in AI Education: How Students Are Rethinking Human Intelligence?

Rekik (2026) — ICLR HCAIR Workshop. 471 students over 6 years show a shift from AI preference to human intelligence preference (65–90% by 2026). Four phases: hype, distrust, trust, dependency. Design-oriented courses accelerate this cognitive shift more than technical courses.
