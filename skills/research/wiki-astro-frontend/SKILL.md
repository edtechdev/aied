# Wiki Astro Frontend

Covers the homepage layout, the SVG concept map, site title/branding copy, and the build→deploy→verify flow. the maintainer iterates on this directly and often — this skill encodes the design preferences and the technical pitfalls.

## Renaming a concept's displayed title (keep the slug)

When the maintainer asks to rename a concept page (e.g. "AI Plagiarism Detection" → "AI Detection", 2026-08-28), change only the **displayed `title:`** and **KEEP the slug** — renaming the slug breaks every `[[slug]]` inline link across articles/concepts, the redirect table, and concept-index references. This matches the established pattern (`student-modeling` slug with title "Learner Modeling and Adaptive Instruction"). To rename the display title, sync it in EVERY place the label appears:
1. `concepts/<slug>.md` — frontmatter `title:` + the lead blockquote's **bold label** + any leading sentence that repeats the old name.
2. `src/data/conceptIndex.ts` — only if the sidebar stores a piped display label (it mostly stores bare slugs, so usually no change).
3. `journal.md` and `index.md` — the `- [[slug]] — <Title>` display lines.
4. `tooling/concept-index.md` — the `- **\`slug\`** — Title` Canonical entry. Do NOT change its `phrases:` alias list (search terms like "AI plagiarism detection" are legitimately kept as findable phrases even after a title change).
5. Any `[[slug|old label]]` piped links in `concepts/*.md` / `articles/*.md` that render the old title (grep `slug\|old name` across `.md`).
6. Bump the concept's `updated:` timestamp and rebuild.

Check the `wiki-inline-links` scanner alias dict (`skills/research/wiki-inline-links/scripts/inline_link_scan.py`) — it maps phrases→slug, so a title change usually needs no scanner edit (e.g. `'plagiarism-detection': ['plagiarism detection', 'ai detection', ...]` already covered the new name). Verify with `grep` that no old display string remains, then build/verify/deploy.

**Rename guardrail — exclude articles whose OWN titles contain the old phrase (2026-08-29).** When you grep for the old display string to verify it's gone, you'll match whole article pages whose titles legitimately contain the phrase (e.g. renaming the `agentic-ai` concept to "Agentic AI" also matched `Agentic AI in Education: A Scoping Review...`, `Agentic AI and Pedagogical Best Practice...`, etc.). Those are separate article pages with their own titles — do NOT touch them. Restrict the check to the concept's display-title locations: `concepts/<slug>.md` frontmatter, `index.md`, `journal.md`, `tooling/concept-index.md`, plus `[[<slug>|old label]]` piped links (note piped links usually render lowercase forms like `agentic AI`/`agents`, which are unaffected by a title change). Only the exact `[[<slug>]] — <Old Title>` entries and the frontmatter `title:` need updating.

**Batch concept creation: delegate page-writing to parallel subagents, register yourself (2026-08-29).** When creating several NEW concept pages at once, delegate each page's WRITING to a parallel subagent (7 ran concurrently) with a strict brief: exact frontmatter (`title`/`created`/`updated`/`type`/`tags`/`confidence`), the `> **Concept name** — synthesis` lead (bold name, NOT literal "Synthesis:"), `##`/`###` sections only (NEVER `####`), and end with `## Connected Concepts` + `## Connected Articles`. Give each subagent: the relevant article slugs to synthesize from (full text at `articles/<slug>.md`, raw at `raw/papers/<slug>.md`), the CONCEPT SLUG ALLOWLIST (all real concept slugs, e.g. from `tooling/concept-index.md`) so they only wikilink to real pages, and the rule to verify every article slug exists. Then YOU handle all registration: sidebar `src/data/conceptIndex.ts` (thematically correct group), `tooling/concept-index.md` (alphabetical + bump `**Total concepts:**`), `index.md` (alphabetical flat list + `Total pages`), `journal.md` (new `## "YYYY-MM-DD` section at top, entries `- ◐ [[slug]] — ''` + a title line, bump `Total entries`), the `concepts/ai-education.md` umbrella Connected Concepts list, and regenerate `llms.txt`/`llms-full.txt`. Validate after: bracket balance, 0 broken links, 0 self-links, sidebar==actual concept count (Python set compare), run `inline_link_scan --apply` then re-verify, run `check_list_formatting.py`. Then build + deploy.

**Batch concept rename also touches `tooling/concept-index.md` + `index.md` display + piped links.** In addition to the frontmatter `title:` + lead-bold, the `index.md` line is `- [[slug]] — Old Title` and `tooling/concept-index.md` is `- **`slug`** — Old Title` — both must be updated to the new display title, and grep for piped links rendering the old phrase (e.g. `[[sustainability|sustainability and AI]]`).

**Batch rename — CLARIFY before acting when two requested titles collide (2026-08-29).** In a 14-rename batch the maintainer listed BOTH "Ethics in AI Education → Ethics" AND "Equity in AI Education → Ethics" — two different concepts can't share the same title. Ask (via `clarify`) rather than guessing or silently re-routing; the resolution was `equity-in-ai-education` → "Equity" (not Ethics). Also note a common pattern in these batches: `trust` → "Trust" broadens to cover BOTH trust-in-AI and interpersonal trust (students↔instructor); keep the body as-is, the title is the only change. And when bulk-fixing piped labels, be careful NOT to rewrite a legitimate phrase that merely contains the old words (e.g. "a six-item **Trust in AI Tools** scale" is an instrument name, not a reference to the concept — leave it).

**Tags are the CONCEPT VOCABULARY (2026-08-29).** `tags` frontmatter must contain ONLY concept slugs (files in `concepts/`). Tags render as clickable chips linking to `/aied/concepts/<slug>/` (all three page templates use `<a class="tag-chip" href={\`/aied/concepts/${tag}/\`}>`). Non-concept metadata that was formerly mixed into `tags` now lives in optional structured fields: `research_method`, `discipline`, `audience`, `level`, `category` (free-form hyphenated/spaced phrases, added to `src/content.config.ts` for all 3 collections as `z.any().transform(...)`). A topic appears BOTH as a concept tag AND in its metadata field where applicable (e.g. tag `meta-analysis-systematic-review` + `research_method: [systematic review]`). To migrate: build a fold map (tag→concept slug) + a tag→field map, then rewrite each page's `tags:` to concept-only and insert the metadata fields AFTER the tags line (strip any pre-existing metadata lines first to avoid duplication — a restore-from-git + re-run can double-insert). Verify 0 non-concept tags remain (Python set check vs `concepts/`), build, deploy.

**Frontmatter migration PITFALL — the `tags:` array can be MULTI-LINE (2026-08-29).** The migration regex `^tags:\s*\[([^\]]*)\]` (and the same regex in the metadata-audit apply step) matches ONLY single-line `tags: [a, b]`. Two files in the repo use the YAML block form `tags:` followed by indented `  - item` lines (`articles/benzion-ai-physics-simulations-virtual-lab.md`, `articles/li-ai-science-situated-learning-teachers-2025.md`). When the regex fails to match those, the script's metadata-field insertion falls back to "insert right after the `tags:` line" — which wedges `research_method: [...]` BETWEEN `tags:` and its indented list items, producing invalid YAML that BREAKS `npm run build` (js-yaml `readBlockMapping` error pointing at the file+line). ALWAYS scan for the block form before a migration/audit apply: `re.search(r'^tags:\s*$', fm, re.M)` → collect those files, normalize them first, and exclude them from the regex-based writer (or handle them specially). After any bulk frontmatter edit, grep for a metadata field line immediately followed by an indented `- ` item — that's the corruption signature.

## Embed mode (?embed) + Embed button (2026-09-04)
Chromeless "embed" mode so visitors can drop a page into an LMS or other site. Two pieces:
- **`?embed` URL param → chromeless + light.** A head `<script is:inline>` in `BaseLayout.astro` runs BEFORE first paint: if the URL matches `/[?&]embed([=&]|$)/` it sets `data-embed=""` on `<html>` and forces `data-theme="light"` (embed always light, regardless of the visitor's saved theme). The anti-flash background rule is extended so `html[data-embed]` is white, and the main theme head script is guarded to NOT override embed's light. CSS in BaseLayout hides the header, footer, both sidebars/rail, nav backdrop, mobile TOC (`.toc-mobile`), prev-next nav (`.prevnext`), the sidebar/hamburger toggles, the page-meta line (star/created/updated/tags — class `page-meta`, added to the meta `<div>` in all three page templates), the `.source-buttons` row, the `.embed-btn`, and the `.page-type-badge`; `.docs-layout` collapses to a single content column and `.middle` gets full width. **CRITICAL: these selectors must be fully `:global([data-embed] ...)`** because several target elements (`.toc-mobile`, `.prevnext`, `.page-meta`, `.source-buttons`, `.embed-btn`) are rendered by CHILD components — Astro scoped CSS in the layout cannot match them (see the Astro-scoping pitfall below). If an element doesn't hide in embed, it's a scope mismatch, not a logic error.
- **Embed button lives INSIDE `SourceButtons.astro`** (refactor 2026-09-04: originally a separate `EmbedButton.astro` component, but Astro scoping meant the child's button did NOT inherit the parent's scoped `.source-pill` style and sat OUTSIDE the `.source-buttons` flex row). ## Workflow for a frontend edit
1. Make the single targeted edit to the `.astro` file.
2. `npm run build` (workdir `<WIKI>`) — confirm `0 errors`. A green push does NOT mean a green deploy.
3. Verify in `dist/` before committing (grep the built HTML for the changed string).
4. `git add -A && git commit && git push`.
5. **Wait for the "Deploy Astro to Pages" workflow to complete, NOT just "Build & Verify".** Poll `gh run list --workflow "Deploy Astro to Pages" --limit 1 --json status,conclusion -q '.[0]'` until `completed/success`, then fetch the live page (Pages lags after green). **Propagation can take ~2–3 minutes, not seconds (2026-08-25):** after the deploy shows `completed/success`, GitHub Pages may keep serving the previous version — a freshly-added page 404s, or a deleted page keeps returning 200, for ~2–3 min even with a cache-busting query param. Sleep ~60–90s and re-check rather than tight-looping; for deletions confirm via the homepage reference count dropping to 0 (`curl -s <site>/ | grep -c "<slug>"`), for additions confirm the target URL flips from 404 to 200.

## Pitfalls
- **Radial SVG with 12+ nodes is ILLEGIBLE on phones.** A 12px label in a 900-unit viewBox scales to ~4px on a 360px screen. **Fix:** wrap the SVG in `.concept-map-wrap` and add a `.concept-map-mobile` flex-wrap `<nav>` with the same links; `@media (max-width:640px){ .concept-map-wrap{display:none} .concept-map-mobile{display:flex} }`. Mobile list uses full-size `1rem` text on large tap targets; center node styled distinctly. Astro minifies the query to `@media (width<=640px)` — grep that form when verifying.
- **`data-astro-cid-*` attributes break standalone SVG rendering.** If you extract the `<svg>` from built `dist/index.html` to render (e.g. with `cairosvg`), strip scope attrs first: `re.sub(r' data-astro-cid-[a-z0-9]+','',svg)` — otherwise the XML parser errors `not well-formed (invalid token)`.
- **`cairosvg` uses a fallback Arial that renders WIDER than the site's real Inter/system-ui stack**, so it falsely reports long labels as "clipped/overflowing". Treat cairosvg label-clipping reads as approximate; cross-check the live page before "fixing" text that isn't actually broken. When a label genuinely overflows a uniform rect, prefer shortening the label (or scaling its font down) over enlarging the rect.
- **Adaptive label font:** keep node rects uniform but scale `font-size` down for long labels (`label.length>=16 → smaller`) so labels don't clip.
- **SVG sent as an image attachment never reaches the agent.** When the maintainer attaches `*.svg` via an image path (e.g. Telegram "Photo"), the platform tries to decode it as a raster (PNG/JPG) and the file is dropped — it does NOT land in `~/.ai-agent/cache/documents/`. Ask him to send it as a **file attachment** ("File"), **paste the markup as text**, or **drop it on disk** and give the path. If he sends a reference SVG with richer styling (icons, arrowheads, shadows), the explicit preferences above (uniform color, no inter-node lines, bigger fonts, tight layout) override the reference.
- **Iterative layout edits are direct and fast.** the maintainer frequently asks to swap node positions, rename labels, or change titles. Make the single targeted change, rebuild, verify, push, and confirm the Deploy workflow — don't batch unrelated frontend changes into one commit.
- **Astro scoped-CSS does NOT cross component boundaries (big one).** Astro scopes each `.astro` component's `<style>` block to that component's own `data-astro-cid-*` attribute. So a style rule written in `BaseLayout.astro`'s `<style>` (e.g. `.recent-block h2[data-astro-cid-<BaseLayout>]`) will NEVER match an `<h2>`/`<ul>`/`<a>` that a CHILD component (`RecentActivity.astro`) renders — the child's elements carry the child's scope id, and the selector silently matches nothing, so the element falls back to global/Pico sizing. **Symptom:** a font-size (or any style) you "fixed" on a component's output doesn't take effect on the live page even though the built CSS shows the rule. **Fix:** put the styles in the `<style>` block of the component that renders the markup, not the parent that includes it. **Verify:** after build, confirm the rendered element carries the same `data-astro-cid-*` as the CSS rule (`grep` the built HTML for `data-astro-cid-<id>>📄 Recently` and the CSS for `.recent-block h2[data-astro-cid-<id>]`).

## Sätteri markdown rendering + math (2026-08-30)
Astro 7 uses **Sätteri** (`@astrojs/markdown-satteri`, wraps the `satteri` package) as its native markdown pipeline. The wiki body renderer lives in **`src/lib/markdown.ts`** and is shared by all three page templates.

- **Body rendering is NOT the default Astro content render** — the templates call `renderMarkdown(body, ...)` from `src/lib/markdown.ts`, which pipes the raw body through `markdownToHtml(...)` from `satteri` with `features: { gfm: true, math: true, frontmatter: true }`. It also handles wikilinks + DOI preprocessing BEFORE Sätteri (see below), and adds heading ids/anchors + TOC collection as Sätteri HAST plugins.
- **Math delimiters are `$...$` (inline) / `$$...$$` (display) — NOT `\(...\)`.** The Sätteri `math` feature only understands `$`/`$$`. `\(...\)` renders as literal backslash text. the maintainer's instruction: "use $$ or $ instead of a backslash". For math inside a prose sentence use `$...$` (inline), NOT `$$` (which forces a display/block). Sätteri emits inline math as `<code class="language-math math-inline">…` and display math as `<pre><code class="language-math math-display">…`.
- **KaTeX is server-rendered in a HAST plugin** (`src/lib/markdown.ts`), NOT client-side: the plugin matches `pre`/`code` with `language-math` classes and returns `{ type: 'raw', value: katex.renderToString(tex, { displayMode: ..., throwOnError: false }) }`. So there is no JS/Katex auto-render; math ships as finished HTML. `katex` is a dependency; `import 'katex/dist/katex.min.css'` in `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro`.
- **Wikilinks are resolved by string preprocessing, not Sätteri's `wikilinks` feature.** Sätteri's own wikilinks feature emits plain `<a href="slug">` nodes indistinguishable from normal links (no base path, no smart title), so `renderMarkdown` expands `[[slug|Label]]`/`[[slug]]` into routed markdown links itself: article > faq > concept priority, with `smartTitle` for bare links, exactly like the old regex renderer.
- **Heading ids + `heading-anchor` links + TOC** are added by a Sätteri HAST plugin using `github-slugger`, and the `blockquote` gets `class="blockquote"` via another HAST plugin (matches the old CSS).
- **Editing tip:** every template must pass its three slug Sets so wikilink routing resolves. If you change the renderer, edit `src/lib/markdown.ts` once — never patch one template's rendering in isolation (they no longer each hold a copy).
- Verify math in built output: `grep -o 'katex-mathml' dist/faqs/<slug>/index.html` should return N matches equal to the number of math expressions, and `grep 'language-math' dist/.../index.html` should return nothing (all converted to KaTeX).

## Deciding whether to create a NEW concept page (the maintainer asks "should this be a concept page?")
the maintainer frequently sends a paper and asks whether it warrants a new concept page (e.g. a sustainability/SDG paper, an AI-sycophancy paper). Use this decision heuristic rather than defaulting to "yes" or "no":
- **Count the cluster FIRST.** `grep` the existing `articles/` + `concepts/` for the topic family (slug + `tags:` + title). ~4–5 articles already touching the topic (or 2–3 tagged `sustainability`/`ai-detection`-style) justify a dedicated concept page; 1 paper alone usually does not — place it in the best existing concept instead. Check whether any article is already tagged with the would-be topic tag (`grep -l "tags: .*<topic>" articles/*.md`) — that's a strong signal a page is warranted.
- **Place by SUBSTANCE, not by framing lens.** A paper that *cites* SDG 4 / Education for Sustainable Development as motivation may in fact be about something else (e.g. a K-12/teacher-education systematic review). Its home is the concept matching its actual content, and the sustainability/SDG framing belongs in a sentence or two — not a reason to force a sustainability concept page. Only create the sustainability concept if a genuine cluster of sustainability-substance articles exists (environmental footprint, SDG pedagogy, sustainable AI design).
- **When you do create it**, follow `references/concept-page-registration.md` (register in sidebar `conceptIndex.ts`, `tooling/concept-index.md` + Total header, `ai-education.md`, reciprocal links, inline-link pass, bump `updated`), and consider whether existing related articles should get reciprocal Connected Articles back-links to it.
- **Authoring pitfall — DON'T self-link a new concept's own slug in its narrative.** When writing a new concept page (e.g. `sustainability.md`), it's easy to write `[[sustainability|Education for Sustainable Development]]` or `[[sustainability|ESD-aligned]]` in the body. Those are self-links to the page's own slug and must be plain text instead. After writing a new concept page, grep the narrative for `[[<own-slug>` and strip them (also true for any article whose slug you reference in its own body). The `wiki-inline-links` `--apply` scanner won't insert these (it skips self-links), but hand-written prose will — check `before split('## Connected')` for `[[<slug>`.
- **Don't wikilink to a concept that doesn't exist yet.** If an article references a topic (e.g. `sustainability`) before its concept page is created, leave it as plain text — a `[[slug]]` to a non-existent concept renders as a broken link and fails the inline-link verify gate. Either create the concept first or keep the term unlinked until it exists.
- Report the recommendation to the maintainer explicitly ("yes, worth a page — N articles cluster around it" vs "this one is better placed under X; the SDG framing is incidental"), and offer to either create the page now or wait for him to send more cluster articles to enrich it.
