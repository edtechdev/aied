**TeacherServer** is a free collection of AI generators aimed at educators, organised into two sets: fifteen most-used tools for K-12 teachers and fifteen for college faculty, inside a total of 1,194 tools. Each tool is a form: you describe what you need, refine the response in conversation, then save or share the result.

## What you can do with it
For K-12: lesson plan, worksheet, IEP goal, math word problem, reading activity, science lesson plan and science experiment generators, a phonics and reading coach, a Lexile score adjuster, a curriculum quiz maker, a historical-perspective generator and tech-ethics scenarios. For college faculty: a text proofreader, paraphraser, case study generator, research idea generator, assignment feedback, discussion prompts and a rubric generator. The site also republishes summaries of recent research on AI in education.

## Notes
The project claims a locally installed AI server for speed and that data is processed locally and never stored, plus Department of Education guideline compliance; the [team page](https://teacherserver.com/index-team.php) names Zafer Unal, a professor at the University of South Florida, as founder and developer, alongside an advisory board of four faculty members from other universities. Those claims are still worth verifying before use with student work. Access requires registration, and there is a companion paid-later platform for building your own tools.
