**EduGems** is a collection of pre-made prompts — "Gems" — for educators using Google Gemini. Each entry has its own page with a description and buttons to use the Gem or make your own copy, so a teacher can run a working prompt in Gemini without writing one from scratch.

## What you can do with it
The library covers planning and classroom work (career caricatures, check-for-understanding activities, conference concierge, CRA math activity, SMART goals, trading cards, form analysis, Classroom and Forms headers, Jeopardy-style games, LETRS lesson planning, micro-inquiry sparks, podcast matchmaking, quiz generation), parent communication, and AI use itself — expectations statements, an AI integration coach, a prompt architect and a prompt doctor, a SAMR activity, an AI-use reflection and a Gem generator. Newest additions are listed on the front page, and teachers can submit their own for inclusion.

## Notes
Curated by Eric Curts, who publishes the accompanying tutorial video, newsletter and wider edtech resources at Control Alt Achieve. Making a copy of a Gem requires a Google account and the Gemini terms come with it, so this is a library to evaluate before pointing students at it, not a tool that can be used anonymously.
