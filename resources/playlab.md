**Playlab** is a creator platform where educators build their own AI apps — chat-based assistants and interactive tools — instead of choosing from someone else's template. An app can be grounded in your own curriculum, rubrics, standards and references rather than the model's general knowledge, and each app runs on a model you pick from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta and others, with the cost visible while you build. Platform knowledge graphs for Illustrative Mathematics, OpenSciEd, state standards and OER Commons come pre-mapped so the AI follows how concepts relate rather than where a keyword appears.

## What you can do with it
Build and remix: start an app from scratch or adapt one shared by another educator in the community gallery (hundreds of apps, including student-built ones). Steer behavior per app — toggle image and voice input, set memory and user profiles, or switch on Avoid Answer Generation so the assistant guides students toward an answer instead of handing it over. Beyond chat, apps can use a document editor with Docx and Google Drive export, Python code execution, math input, and image processing. Teachers can review any student conversation, and automated flags can pause a conversation and notify the teacher directly. Rosters connect through Clever, ClassLink or Google SSO.

## Who it is for
K-12 teachers and districts that want classroom controls and curriculum grounding without building infrastructure, university instructors and instructional designers who want a custom tool for one course, and students aged 13 and over (with guardian permission under 18) who build apps themselves rather than only using them. The organization is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit; it states that educator accounts are free in perpetuity and that school and district tiers are paid partnerships with learning engineers and workshops attached.

## Notes
Access to the builder is currently gated: the get-started page asks for an email address and a spot in an onboarding workshop, or an invite code. The platform's own claims — FERPA, COPPA, GDPR and CCPA alignment, SOC 2 Type II and annual penetration tests on request, your work never used to train outside models, and output rights assigned to the creator — are published on the site and, where they matter for a district decision, worth verifying in the data processing agreement rather than taking from the marketing page. Its capability comparison against MagicSchool, ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude is Playlab's own and carries a "current as of mid-2026" date. Playlab also points out in its FAQ what any generative tool requires of its user: output can be wrong, so check for bias, verify accuracy and avoid entering personally identifiable information. It was co-founded by Yusuf Ahmad (CEO) and Ian Serlin (CTO), and grew out of end-user programming work with links to Scratch and the MIT Media Lab.
